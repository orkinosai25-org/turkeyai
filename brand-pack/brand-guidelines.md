## 🎯 Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** TurkiyAI Holidays  
**Domain:** turkiyeai.travel  
**Tagline:** AI Knows Turkey Better  
**Endorsement:** Powered by OrkinosAI

### What We Are
TurkiyAI Holidays is an AI-powered Turkish travel discovery and planning platform. We provide intelligent, personalized travel recommendations for destinations across Türkiye, focusing on the Mediterranean coast and iconic locations like Bodrum, Antalya, Cappadocia, Fethiye, and Marmaris.

### What We Are NOT
- ❌ Not a tour operator or travel agency
- ❌ We do not take payments or issue tickets
- ❌ We do not book travel on behalf of users
- ✅ We provide AI-powered recommendations and redirect to licensed providers

### Target Audience
- **Primary:** International travelers (25-55), female-skewed, interested in Turkey
- **Secondary:** Families, couples, solo travelers
- **Tertiary:** Travel agents and DMCs (B2B future)

### Brand Personality
- **Premium** but accessible
- **Trustworthy** and expert
- **Warm** and welcoming
- **Modern** and tech-forward
- **Calm** and reassuring
- **Culturally authentic** without stereotypes

## 📚 Brand Story

### The Orkinos Connection

**Orkinos** means "tuna" in Turkish – a creature symbolizing:
- **Intelligence:** Smart navigation and hunting strategies
- **Speed:** Rapid movement through water
- **Navigation:** Natural wayfinding abilities across vast oceans

Just as the tuna navigates the azure Aegean waves, TurkiyAI Holidays helps travelers intelligently navigate their journey through Türkiye.

### Azure Inspiration

Built on **Microsoft Azure**, our platform draws inspiration from:
- **Azure/Aegean blue waters** of the Turkish coast
- **Whitewashed architecture** of Bodrum and coastal towns
- **Mediterranean light** and warmth
- **Ancient Greek-Anatolian heritage** of the region

### Cultural DNA: Bodrum

Our visual identity is deeply rooted in Bodrum and the Aegean coast:
- **Whitewashed limestone houses** → Purity, light, calm
- **Aegean & azure blues** → Sea, sky, trust
- **Olive trees** → Wisdom, longevity, land
- **Bougainvillea flowers** → Joy, femininity, life
- **Ancient terracotta ceramics** → Heritage, continuity
- **Orange blossom scent** → Warmth, hospitality

Our visual system balances:

### Color Usage Rules

**DO:**
✅ Use white + blue + turquoise as the main canvas  
✅ Use olive & sage for filters and calm UI areas  
✅ Use bougainvillea + coral for CTAs and highlights  
✅ Use terracotta sparingly for section dividers and quotes  
✅ Keep backgrounds light and airy

**DON'T:**
❌ Don't flood pages with pink or red  
❌ Don't use neon or overly saturated colors  
❌ Don't mix too many accents in one screen  
❌ Don't use political or religious color associations

### WCAG Accessibility

All color combinations must meet **WCAG AA standards** (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text).

**Approved high-contrast combinations:**
- Aegean Blue (#1F6FAF) on White → 7.2:1 ✅
- Warm Charcoal (#1F1D1B) on Limestone White → 14.5:1 ✅
- Warm Slate 700 (#423E3A) on White → 9.8:1 ✅

**Avoid:**
- Blush Pink on white (insufficient contrast)
- Orange Blossom on white (use only for non-text elements)

## ✍️ Typography

### Primary Font: Inter

**Rationale:** Modern, clean, excellent readability, multilingual support (including Turkish characters), open-source.

**Font stack:**
```css
font-family: Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif;
```

### Font Sizes

| Size | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| xs | 12px | Tiny labels, captions |
| sm | 14px | Small text, metadata |
| base | 16px | Body text (default) |
| lg | 18px | Large body, lead text |
| xl | 20px | H5 |
| 2xl | 24px | H4 |
| 3xl | 30px | H3 |
| 4xl | 36px | H2 |
| 5xl | 48px | H1 |
| 6xl | 60px | Display |
| 7xl | 72px | Hero headlines |

### Font Weights

- **Light (300):** Decorative use only
- **Normal (400):** Body text, descriptions
- **Medium (500):** Emphasized body text, labels
- **Semibold (600):** Headings, subheadings
- **Bold (700):** Strong emphasis, hero headlines

### Typography Hierarchy

#### H1 - Hero Headlines
```
Size: 48px (5xl)
Weight: 600 (Semibold)
Color: Warm Charcoal
Letter spacing: -0.5px
```

#### H2 - Section Headers
```
Size: 36px (4xl)
Weight: 600
Color: Warm Charcoal
```

#### H3 - Subsection Headers
```
Size: 30px (3xl)
Weight: 600
Color: Warm Slate 800
```

#### Body Text
```
Size: 16px (base)
Weight: 400
Color: Warm Slate 700
Line height: 1.5 (24px)
```

#### Small Text / Captions
```
Size: 14px (sm)
Weight: 400
```

## 🎯 Design Principles

### 1. Mediterranean Calm
- Use whitespace generously
- Avoid visual clutter
- Create breathing room around elements
- Embrace simplicity

### 2. Warm & Welcoming
- Use soft colors and rounded corners
- Include human-friendly microcopy
- Show personality without being unprofessional
- Make AI feel approachable

### 3. Culturally Authentic
- Reference Turkish/Mediterranean heritage respectfully
- Avoid tourist clichés (flags, kebabs, generic landmarks)
- Use authentic color palette rooted in real locations
- Respect Turkish language and naming conventions

### 4. Premium but Accessible
- High-quality imagery and design
- Professional execution
- Not intimidating or exclusive
- Welcoming to all travelers

### 5. Feminine-Friendly Design
- Use warm, inviting colors
- Soften hard edges
- Include emotional warmth
- Appeal to women without stereotyping

### 6. AI-Native Experience
- Showcase AI capabilities clearly
- Make intelligence visible
- Provide smart, contextual suggestions
- Build trust through transparency

## 💬 Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Attributes

**Expert but Approachable**
- We know Turkey intimately
- We share knowledge generously
- We never talk down to users
- We make complex planning simple

**Warm & Friendly**
- We're like a knowledgeable local friend
- We're enthusiastic about Turkey
- We're patient and helpful
- We celebrate the joy of travel

**Trustworthy & Professional**
- We're accurate with information
- We set realistic expectations
- We're transparent about limitations
- We prioritize user needs

**Modern & Intelligent**
- We leverage AI naturally
- We're efficient and helpful
- We adapt to user preferences
- We stay current

### Tone Variations by Context

**Homepage / Marketing:** Enthusiastic, inviting, inspiring  
**Search / Browse:** Helpful, clear, organized  
**AI Chat:** Friendly, knowledgeable, conversational  
**Errors:** Apologetic, helpful, solution-oriented  
**Confirmations:** Positive, clear, reassuring

### Language Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use "Türkiye" (not "Turkey")
- Say "your trip" or "your journey"
- Be conversational: "Let's explore..."
- Use emojis sparingly: 🏖️ ✨ 🗺️
- Ask questions: "What kind of experience interests you?"

**DON'T:**
- Use travel agent jargon
- Say "book now" (we don't book)
- Use "customers" (say "travelers")
- Over-use exclamation marks
- Be too formal or stiff

## ✅ Do's and Don'ts

### Logo Usage

**DO:**
✅ Use approved logo files from brand pack  
✅ Maintain clear space around logo  
✅ Use appropriate logo variant for context  
✅ Ensure logo is legible at all sizes  
✅ Include "Powered by OrkinosAI" lockup when required

**DON'T:**
❌ Distort, stretch, or modify logo  
❌ Change logo colors outside approved variants  
❌ Add effects (shadows, glows, outlines)  
❌ Place logo on busy backgrounds  
❌ Separate mark from wordmark without permission

### Color Usage

**DO:**
✅ Follow color usage guidelines  
✅ Maintain WCAG contrast ratios  
✅ Use colors intentionally and meaningfully  
✅ Keep backgrounds light and clean

**DON'T:**
❌ Use random colors outside the palette  
❌ Create neon or oversaturated variations  
❌ Mix too many accent colors on one screen  
❌ Use low-contrast text combinations

### Imagery

**DO:**
✅ Use high-quality photography  
✅ Show authentic Turkish locations  
✅ Feature diverse travelers  
✅ Capture Mediterranean light and colors  
✅ Show real experiences, not staged tourism

**DON'T:**
❌ Use generic stock photos  
❌ Show tourist clichés  
❌ Include dated or low-quality images  
❌ Feature flags or political symbols  
❌ Use images with competing brand watermarks

### Writing & Messaging

**DO:**
✅ Write in clear, friendly language  
✅ Use "Türkiye" (Turkish spelling)  
✅ Be helpful and informative  
✅ Show personality  
✅ Respect Turkish culture

**DON'T:**
❌ Use travel industry jargon  
❌ Make booking promises  
❌ Over-use exclamation marks  
❌ Be overly salesy  
❌ Use cultural stereotypes
